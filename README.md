## 💡 Key Dataset Insights

### 1. 📈 Revenue Trajectory & Yearly Trend
* **Historical Peak:** Sales reached a peak in **2024 ($1.5M)** and maintained strong performance in **2025 ($1.4M)**.
* **Current Trend:** Revenue shows **$0.5M** recorded for **2026**, highlighting steady ongoing platform activity across years.

### 2. 📦 Product Category Revenue Breakdown
* **Top Revenue Driver:** **Electronics** leads all categories by a wide margin, generating **$1.8M** (~60% of overall sales revenue).
* **Supporting Categories:** 
  * **Accessories:** $0.6M
  * **Home Office:** $0.4M
  * **Wearables:** $0.4M
  * **Gaming:** $0.2M
  * **Stationery:** $0.1M

### 3. 👥 Customer Segmentation Breakdown
* **Regular Customers:** Form the majority of total revenue at **55.5% ($2M)**.
* **VIP Customers:** Account for **34.72% ($1M)** of revenue, demonstrating strong high-value customer engagement.
* **New Customers:** Represent **9.69%** of sales volume, indicating consistent new customer acquisition.

### 4. 🚚 Order Fulfillment & Status Performance
* **Fulfillment Rate:** **45K orders (91.8%)** were successfully **Completed**.
* **Cancellations & Returns:** 
  * **Cancelled Orders:** 2K orders (~4.1%).
  * **Returned Orders:** 1K orders (~2.0%).
* High completion rates confirm solid operational delivery reliability.

### 5. 🗺️ Regional Distribution & Payment Channels
* **Geographic Reach:** Concentrated sales footprint across key urban centers (Tehran, Karaj, Rasht, Mashhad, etc.).
* **Primary Payment Gateway:** **Gateway payments** represent the dominant choice with **23.4K orders**, followed by **Card-to-Card (11.7K orders)**.

## 🗂️ Data Architecture & Model Schema

The project processes data across five key tables:

* `clean_final_data`: Central transformed dataset for unified report visuals.
* `customers`: Profile details and customer segmentation groups.
* `orders`: Order dates, quantities, and fulfillment status logs.
* `payments`: Payment method channels and transactional records.
* `products`: Product catalog attributes, prices, and categories.
